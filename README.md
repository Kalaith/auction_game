# Auction House Tycoon

Auction House Tycoon is a property strategy game about buying under pressure, protecting your margin, renovating carefully, and selling before costs erase the deal.

Winning an auction is not automatically good. The real challenge is knowing what a property is worth, when to stop bidding, and whether a renovation will improve the deal or just make a loss prettier.

## Gameplay

- Study market conditions and weekly listings.
- Inspect properties and estimate risk.
- Set a walk-away price before bidding.
- Compete against other buyers in live auctions.
- Renovate, hold, or sell purchased properties.
- Track cash, debt, fees, repairs, and net worth.

## Goal

Grow your portfolio and net worth without letting bad bids, holding costs, or over-renovation destroy your cashflow.

## Controls

- Mouse: inspect listings, bid, renovate, and sell.

## Current Scope

Playable auction-to-resale loop with market pulse, bidding pressure, renovations, sale outcomes, and campaign progress.

Properties, bidder profiles, upgrades, and market events are data-driven from
